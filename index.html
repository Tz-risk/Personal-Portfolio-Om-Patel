Endpoint defense · Complete

SessionSentinel

A two-layer defense against session-token theft. A Chrome extension clears authentication cookies when a tab closes. A host daemon watches Chrome’s cookie database and stops unauthorized readers.

  • Manifest V3 service worker maps open tabs and purges cookies for that host on close.
  • Python daemon (watchdog and psutil) terminates unauthorized readers of the Chrome SQLite cookie store in under 500 ms.
  • Canonical path checks keep VS Code, Discord, and Spotify from being treated as stealers.
  • Checked against a local proof-of-concept reader, rogue_stealer.py. Windows and Linux are both complete.
